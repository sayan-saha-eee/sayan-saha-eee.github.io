About Me

Hi, I am Sayan Saha, and I am final-year EEE graduate at VIT. I have developed a deep interest in Power Electronics, Control Systems and Embedded Systems. I enjoy going beyond the theory and figuring out how things work in the practical world. My interest in power electronics led me to an internship under Dr. Anindya Dasgupta at PEDS lab, IIST. There, I worked on a DC microgid with a four-switch buck-boost converter and evaluated the LMG5200 GaN evaluation board. Before that, I spend six months at Xibotix, working on embedded systems and developing a control system for a microfluidic syringe pump. I was also involved in acquisitions and PCB design. My exposure to an industrial setting was during summer of 2025, when I completed a training programme at WBPDCL.This experience helped me appreciate the scale and complexity of power generation.
