About Me

Hi, I am Sayan Saha, and I am currently a final year Electrical and Electronics Engineering undergraduate at Vellore Institute of Technology, Vellore, India. I have developed a deep interest in Power Electronics, Control Systems, and Embedded Systems.

Internships

  • At IIST Thiruvananthapuram, as a summer research intern at the Avionics Department under Dr. Anindya Dasgupta, I modeled a four-switch buck-boost (FSBB) converter for DC microgrids. I also analyzed the LMG5200 power stage using the evaluation board to study GaN devices.

  • At Xibotix Pvt. Ltd., while doing my 6-month internship, I learned designing circuits and PCBs. I also developed control algorithms for a microfluidic syringe pump. I was also involved in component selection and procurement.

  • At WBPDCL, as a trainee, I studied thermal power plant operations, maintenance scenarios, and standard industrial safety practices through on-site visits and the Bakreshwar power plant simulator.
