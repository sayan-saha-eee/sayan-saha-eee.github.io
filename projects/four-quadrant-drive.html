Four-Quadrant DC Motor Drive

Introduction

A hardware implementation of a Class E DC chopper circuit to achieve four-quadrant control of a permanent magnet DC (PMDC) motor. The system enables forward motoring, reverse motoring, forward braking, and reverse braking. It utilizes Pulse Width Modulation (PWM) for precise speed regulation and directional control. This was a team project with Harshak V P.

Class E Chopper

A Class E chopper is capable of bidirectional power flow, enabling full four-quadrant motor operation. The operating modes are defined as follows:

  • First Quadrant (Forward Motoring): The chopper acts as a step-down converter where both output voltage and load current are positive.
  • Second Quadrant (Forward Braking): The chopper behaves as a step-up converter with positive output voltage and negative load current.
  • Third Quadrant (Reverse Motoring): The motor is connected with opposite polarity, resulting in negative output voltage and negative load current.
  • Fourth Quadrant (Reverse Braking): The output voltage is negative while the load current is positive.

Circuit Overview

The hardware architecture is divided into control, driver, and power stages. In the control stage, an ATmega328P microcontroller reads inputs from three push buttons for quadrant selection and a 10 kΩ potentiometer for analog speed control. It generates the necessary PWM logic while maintaining a 100 µs dead time during transitions to prevent shoot-through short conditions.

In the driver stage, two IR2110 MOSFET gate driver ICs independently drive the high and low-side MOSFETs of the H-bridge. A bootstrap network utilizing a 0.22µF capacitor and a ultra-fast recovery diode provides the required high-side gate voltages without switching delays.

The power stage consists of a full H-bridge topology constructed with four IRFZ44N N-channel power MOSFETs. These MOSFETs are used to directly drive a 12V permanent magnet DC motor.

Components

  • Arduino ATmega328P: Microcontroller for control logic and PWM generation.
  • IR2110: MOSFET gate driver ICs for driving the high and low-side MOSFETs.
  • IRFZ44N: N-Channel power MOSFETs used for the full H-bridge topology.
  • DC Motor: 12V permanent magnet DC (PMDC) motor.
  • UF4007: Ultra-fast recovery diodes for the bootstrap circuit.
  • Passives: Resistors (10 Ω) and capacitors (0.1µF, 0.22µF, 22µF, 100µF).
  • Inputs: 10 kΩ potentiometer for speed control and push buttons for quadrant selection.
