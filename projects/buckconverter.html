Programmable Synchronous Buck Converter

Introduction

A buck converter is a DC-DC power converter that steps down a higher DC input voltage to a lower DC output voltage. In a typical buck converter, there is one switch and one freewheeling diode. In a synchronous buck converter,there is a controlled switch , instead of a freewheeling diode. It offers higher efficiency although comes at a cost of complexity.

In this project, I designed a synchronous buck converter using the IR2104 half-bridge driver and IRLB8743 MOSFETs. This converter was very useful for me as I could use it as parts of other projects.

Synchronous Buck Converter

As discussed, a synchronous buck converter is an efficient version of the typical buck converter to step down DC voltage. As a switch-mode power regulator, it provides high operating efficiency by replacing the traditional freewheeling diode with an active switch. The converter consists of two semiconductor switches (SW1 and SW2), input and output filter capacitors, and a power inductor.

The primary switch (SW1) is driven by a pulse-width modulation (PWM) signal generated by the microcontroller, while the secondary switch (SW2) operates complementarily to conduct freewheeling current with lower conduction losses. An output LC filter smooths the chopped voltage to deliver a steady DC output with minimal ripple. By dynamically adjusting the PWM duty cycle, the microcontroller regulates the output voltage and current to match the target load requirements.

Technical Details

A synchronous buck converter replaces the traditional freewheeling diode with a low-side MOSFET, significantly reducing conduction losses and improving high-current efficiency.

Operation & Limitations: Proper gate driving and dead-time control are critical to avoid shoot-through. The IR2104 handles this with a fixed 500ns dead time. While functioning as expected, the fixed dead-time and complementary switching limit control flexibility. Additionally, reverse current can flow when output voltage exceeds input voltage —a known topology limitation which makes the control tricky.

Components

  • IR2104 - Half-Bridge Gate Driver (IR2110 can also be used for greater control flexibility)
  • IRLB8743 - N-Channel MOSFETs (high-side and low-side)
  • Inductor - 100 µH, 2.4 A (toroidal core)
  • Capacitors - 10 µF (1), 100 nF (1), 470 µF (2)
  • Diode - SS34 Schottky diode (for faster MOSFET turn-off)
  • Resistors - 10 kΩ (2), 100 kΩ (2), 47 Ω (2)
  • ESP32 - for PWM generation.
