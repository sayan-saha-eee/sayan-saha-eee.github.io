High-Gain Cubic Boost Converter

Introduction

In this project, my teammate Harshak V P and I are developing a novel high-gain non-isolated cubic boost converter. This converter is designed to give a better gain than a conventional boost converter. For a conventional boost converter, to achieve a similar output voltage, a higher duty cycle is required.

Novel High-Gain Non-Isolated DC-DC Converter

This converter steps up a 30 V DC input to a 400 V DC output and is designed for a output power of 200 W. It utilizes a single switch, three inductors, and a capacitive voltage-lift network. In the PSIM simulation, we achieved a high voltage conversion ratio of 13.33x at a duty ratio of 42.17%.

Development

I am currently working on this project and these are the developments so far. After designing the PCB in KiCad, we etched and drilled our own PCB since commercial fabrication would have cost well over 5000 INR. The inductors were hand-wound to meet the custom specifications.
