LC Meter

Introduction

This project is about building a low-cost digital LC meter using an Arduino to measure the inductance and capacitance of basic electrical components. It's a fairly simple project that has been built by many electronics enthusiasts, so the goal wasn't to create something new or complex, but to have some fun, and build a useful little tool.

Capacitance Measurement

The method opted for the measurement of unknown capacitor (Cx) is based on the transient response of the RC circuit. In an RC circuit, when the DC voltage (Vin) is applied through the resistor (R, whose value is known), the voltage across the capacitor, Vc(t) rises according to (1)

Vc(t) = Vin(1 - e-t/RC)     -------(1)

Time constant for capacitor is the time it takes for voltage Vc(t) to rise to 63.2% of the full charge voltage. The larger the capacitor, the larger is the time constant due to the fact they take more time to charge. In equation (1), RC is the time constant TC. We can find the value of the unknown capacitance for the formula. Since the value of the resistance is already known, we can obtain the capacitance as

Cx = TC / R     --------(2)

Inductance Measurement

For inductance measurement, the methodology adopted was observation of resonant frequency of LC tank circuit. When an inductor is placed parallel to a capacitor and a voltage is provided the circuit rings at a frequency. This frequency is unique for each circuit and is the resonant frequency. Ideally the resonance should continue indefinitely, but the presence of resistance acts as a damping force and the oscillation decays.

An external op-amp was used to condition the analog signal and transform into a digital signal so that the microcontroller can calculate the frequency and hence the inductance. The resonant frequency given by (3)

f = 1 / (2π√(LC))     --------(3)

A capacitor of known value is used with the unknown inductor and the frequency is observed. The inductance can then be computed from the given equation.

Components

  • IR2104 - Half-Bridge Gate Driver (IR2110 can also be used for greater control flexibility)
  • Arduino Nano - Microcontroller
  • Op-Amp - LM339
  • Capacitors - 0.47 µF (ceramic, 2), 100 nF (1), 470 µF (2)
  • Diode - 1N4007 (1)
  • Resistors - 10 kΩ (1), 150 Ω (1), 220 Ω (1), 330 Ω (2)
  • Display - 16x2 LCD.
